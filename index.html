Audio calling + translated captions · Prototype v1

Prototype limitations: This makes a real browser audio call and can translate speech into captions using browser speech recognition plus a translation service. It does not yet replace the caller's voice with a dubbed voice in the remote call. Do not use for emergencies or confidential conversations.

🔐 Privacy & safety

Use only with the other caller's knowledge and consent. This prototype uses a third-party peer-signalling service and a public translation endpoint; speech recognition may also use browser-vendor services. Do not enter passwords, financial information, medical details or other sensitive content. A production version needs a privacy policy, secure backend, explicit consent, abuse controls and a paid translation/voice provider.
